## What is Maths Explore?

Maths Explore is a webapp for engineering calculations. You place blocks of maths, text and diagrams anywhere on paginated sheets (A4 or US Letter). Everything evaluates in reading order against one shared scope, so results update the moment you change an input. Think spreadsheet, except the maths reads like maths.

## First steps

You don't need an account to start. The [sample calcsheets](https://www.mathsexplore.com/samples) open straight in the workspace, so you can change an input and watch every result follow. Each one opens read-only; press **Unlock to edit** when you want to experiment. Free on every plan, trial or no trial.

When you're ready for your own work, every new account starts with a **14-day free trial** of the Professional plan (no card required). Head to [Getting started](guides/getting-started/) to create an account and build your first calcsheet, then work through the guides in the sidebar for blocks, syntax, units, templates and the rest.
